# PhantOS Support

## Get help or report a problem

[Open an issue](https://github.com/giossaurus/phantos/issues) on GitHub. Say which iPhone or iPad and iOS version you use, what you ran, and what happened.

## Common questions

**How do I install software?**
Run `apk add <package>`, for example `apk add python3`. Packages come from Alpine Linux.

**How do I open another shell?**
Tap + at the top of the screen. Hold a tab to rename it or close it. With a hardware keyboard, ⌘T opens a tab and ⌘W closes one.

**How do I change the colors or font?**
Open Settings with the gear button above the keyboard, then Appearance.

**Where are my files?**
Inside the Linux filesystem, and in the Files app under PhantOS.

**Something broke after I changed the launch or boot command.**
Open the iOS Settings app, find PhantOS, and use the reset switch there.
